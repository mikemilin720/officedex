3. Ink-black payoff

Use the dark primary color only for the strongest payoff and CTA moments. This creates contrast without changing the product identity.

Motion and styling rules

  • Transitions: paper panels slide, crop, and unfold; avoid gaming-style light streaks.
  • Type: Plus Jakarta Sans for headlines, Inter for captions and UI labels.
  • Accents: purple identifies OfficeDex; teal guides interaction; green only indicates success or connection.
  • Shadows: soft editorial depth, matching the real app cards and windows.
